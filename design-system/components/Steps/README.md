The three steps of the service: Discover, Implement, Run. Always these three, in this order [J11].

Three white cards in a row, stacking on phones. Each has its number in the `step-number` style with the logo's green-to-blue gradient [M15], a hand-inked line drawing, a small note on the right ("Fixed fee · read-only", "Optional"), a title at 24px, one sentence, three bullets, and a "You get:" line.

The gradient on the numbers is the one place a fade is used on a light ground. Its weakest point is 5.3:1, which is enough for text this large and not for body text [S1].

You provide: nothing; the three steps are fixed. Wording changes need a partner.

Don't: add a fourth step; reorder them; promise a result in the "You get:" line.
