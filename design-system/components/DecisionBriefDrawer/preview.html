1Executive verdict

Move $2,900 of the $4,100 quarterly social budget out of broad social retargeting. Keep Search and Local Map spend intact.

In this sample, broad retargeting produced none of the 9 social sales that could be confirmed. The $1,200 in social prospecting that did produce sales stays, under the holdout test in section 3.

2Key reconciliation finding

Meta reported 148 leads. 139 of them did not match an invoice within 90 days. Two causes account for all 139:

Why 139 of 148 Meta-claimed leads failed invoice matching

3Recommended 4-week holdout and plumbing fix

  1. Offline conversion feed. Send closed sales from the CRM back to Google and Meta, so both optimize toward paying customers, not form fills.
  2. Geographic holdout. Pause social retargeting in 2 of 6 service zones for 4 weeks and compare closed sales against the other 4.
  3. AI crawler access. Unblock OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt and the web application firewall, so ChatGPT search can read the service pages.
