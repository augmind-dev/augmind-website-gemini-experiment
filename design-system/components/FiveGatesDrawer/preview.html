The 5 gates where businesses drop out

When an AI assistant searches the web to answer a buyer, it typically passes five gates. A business can drop out at any one of them. Answers also change from one ask to the next, so we ask each buyer question several times.

Example: six businesses could answer one buyer question.
  1. 1

    Rewrite

    The assistant turns the buyer’s question into a few web searches of its own.

    OutWhere you drop out: None of your pages answer the searches it writes.

    5 of 6 still in

    The fix: Pages that answer the questions buyers actually ask, in their words.

  2. 2

    Search

    It runs those searches against the index it uses.

    OutWhere you drop out: Your site isn’t in the results it gets back.

    4 of 6 still in

    The fix: Indexing, listings, and the sources assistants lean on in your category.

  3. 3

    Read

    It opens a handful of pages to read.

    OutWhere you drop out: A firewall or robots.txt rule blocks its crawler.

    3 of 6 still in

    The fix: Let AI search crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot read your public pages.

  4. 4

    Write

    It writes a short answer from the passages it read.

    OutWhere you drop out: No passage on your site states clearly what you do, where, and for whom.

    2 of 6 still in

    The fix: Plain, quotable passages: services, service area, proof, and prices where you can.

  5. 5

    Cite

    It names some businesses and links a few sources.

    No linkWhere you drop out: You’re named but not linked, or a competitor is linked instead.

    2 named, 1 linked

    The fix: Consistent facts across your site and listings, so the assistant can cite you.

Ask again and the list can change.
