# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026. Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AugMind, LLC ("AugMind," "we," "us" or "our") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit augmind.ai (the "Site"), contact us or request a consultation. Our work is built on a simple idea: your data belongs to you. We apply that same principle to the information you share with us.

## 1. Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you book a conversation or contact us, we collect what you provide, such as your name, work email address, company and your message. If you book a conversation, the booking is made through Google Calendar, and we receive the name, email address, date and time you enter there.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, the Site and our hosting provider collect basic technical information when you visit, such as your browser type, device type, pages viewed, referring page and approximate location derived from your IP address. This is collected through standard server logs, not through cookies set by the Site.

Information from client engagements. When a company engages AugMind, any data it shares with us, such as ad platform exports or sales records, is governed by a separate written agreement and a data-handling record that states why each field is needed, where it is held, who can see it and how long it is kept. This Privacy Policy does not replace those agreements.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To respond to your questions and schedule and hold consultations.

- To prepare quotes, proposals and agreements you ask for.

- To operate, maintain, secure and improve the Site.

- To send you information you have asked for.

- To meet our legal obligations and protect our rights.

## 3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only:

- With service providers that help us run our business, such as website hosting, scheduling, email and file storage providers, who may use it only to provide services to us.

- With professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants, where needed.

- When required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of AugMind, our clients or others.

- In connection with a merger, sale or reorganization of our business, subject to this Policy.

## 4. Cookies

The Site sets no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and it stores nothing in your browser. If we add analytics or similar tools in the future, we will update this Policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

## 5. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, or as required by law. Inquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted when they are no longer needed. Client engagement data is kept according to the retention terms in the client's agreement and data-handling record.

## 6. How We Protect Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including limiting access to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to receive a copy, to correct it, to delete it and to opt out of certain uses. To make a request, book a conversation at [augmind.ai](https://www.augmind.ai/scheduling) and tell us at the start that it is a privacy request, or write to us at the address below. We will verify your request and respond within the time the law requires. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

## 8. Children

The Site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Links to Other Sites

The Site may link to other websites, such as Google Calendar for booking. Their privacy practices are their own, and we encourage you to read their policies.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above. Material changes will be highlighted on the Site.
