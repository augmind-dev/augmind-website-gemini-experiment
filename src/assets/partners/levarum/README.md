Levarum logo files. `levarum-lockup.svg` (grained mark, wordmark with the lifted L) at 48px and up; `-inverse` on dark; `levarum-lockup-small.svg` and `-small-inverse` (flat mark, plain wordmark) below 48px including nav and footer; `-mono` one color; `-stacked` where width is tight; `levarum-mark.svg` alone only beside the name; `levarum-tile.svg` for app icons; `levarum-favicon.svg` at 16–48px. `levarum-wordmark.svg` and `-wordmark-inverse` are the name alone, for places the mark is already present or cannot fit; `levarum-mark-inverse.svg` is the mark alone on dark; `levarum-mark-mono.svg` is the one-color mark; `levarum-tile-inverse.svg` is the app icon on dark. `levarum-misuse.svg` shows what not to do.
`levarum-lockup-tagline.svg` and `-tagline-inverse` add the tagline "Relevant, by design." (Figtree 500, outlined); use at 64px tall or more. All logo files are fixed-color: plain files on light grounds only, `-inverse` files on dark grounds (the dark-theme page, the inverse band in light).
