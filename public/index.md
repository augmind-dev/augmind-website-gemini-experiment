## The truth gap: your ad platforms grade their own homework

Dashboards count clicks and form fills as wins. Your sales records count paying customers. Every reported lead is sorted into one of three outcomes:

- Confirmed sale: matched to a paid invoice (for example, a search web form matched 12 days later).
- Person reviews: a probable match a person checks (for example, same phone, different billing name).
- No sale found: nothing in 90 days of sales records.

## Questions

### How is this different from my agency?

When the team that runs your ads also reports on them, it is grading its own homework. We match every reported lead against your sales records first, keep confirmed sales apart from platform claims, and test any change against a holdout before budget moves. Then we make the changes ourselves, or hand them to your team or agency.

### Will you change anything in our accounts during Discover?

No. Discover is read-only. We look at your ad accounts and sales records and change nothing. Changes happen only in Implement or Run, only if you approve them in writing, and only in accounts you own.

### What do you need from us?

Read access to your Google and Meta ad accounts, an export or read access to your CRM, billing, or scheduling records for the last 90 days, and an hour with the person who knows how a lead becomes a sale in your business.

### What if the Decision Brief says to change nothing?

Then that is the answer, and you can stop there. Discover is a standalone, fixed-fee step. You keep the Brief, the Roadmap, and every account and formula. Implement and Run are optional.

### Can you guarantee AI will name my business?

No, and nobody honestly can. No one controls what an AI assistant says. A measurement is a sample, not a prediction. What we can do is fix what's in your control (whether AI crawlers can read your site, clear answers on your pages, your business listings and reviews) and measure again.
