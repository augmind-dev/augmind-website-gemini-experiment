## Why some providers lost sight of their marketing

Ad platforms measure campaigns with tracking pixels: small pieces of code on your website. On a page for booking a specialist, a pixel can tell an ad platform which page a visitor viewed, and that can reveal something about their health.

Since 2023, federal regulators have warned hospitals and telehealth providers about tracking technology on their websites. Some providers removed their pixels. That was the careful thing to do, but it also left them unable to tell which campaigns bring in patients.

AugMind for Healthcare measures from your own records instead of the visitor's browser.

1. A visitor opens a specialist booking page. For example, an oncology or behavioral health appointment page.
2. A pixel reports the page view to the ad platform. The page address alone can hint at a health condition.
3. AugMind works from your records instead. Campaign results are matched to bookings internally, with no pixel on the page.

## Four safeguards for healthcare engagements

1. Measured against internal records. Campaigns are compared with your scheduling or billing records, not with data collected in the visitor's browser.
2. Platform health settings applied. Where Google and Meta offer health-related restrictions for advertisers, we apply them before anything runs.
3. Privacy, security and legal reviews before access. We access nothing until your privacy, security, and legal teams have reviewed and approved the approach.
4. Physician-led privacy design. Saima Anis, MD, MPH, shapes how each engagement handles data, from the first request to the final report.
